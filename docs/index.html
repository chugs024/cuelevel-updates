    A quiet guest, brought into line.

    Even out finds stretches more than 3 dB louder or quieter than the rest of the cue and turns them back in the nearest pause. No fades, no pumping, no compression. Songs are left as they are.

    Or take the fader yourself.

    Edit mode is a clip gain editor. Drag a section up or down, trim the silence, set fades, mute a cough, drop a marker. Every change shows its value in dB, and undo goes all the way back.

    Lossless, and you can check.

    Built for the person at the desk.

    Drop a whole show

    Drop a folder of audio and video. Levelled copies land in Desktop › CueLevel › Levelled, named after the original with _LVL-18.

    Checks the source

    Clipping, DC offset, left and right out of phase, silence at the ends and extra audio tracks, explained in plain words.

    Knows a song from a speech

    Each cue is Voice, Music or Music + voice. Music is never evened out, and cues that play straight through like a song are left as they are.

    Learns what good sounds like

    Mark finished cues as good. CueLevel keeps their measurements and checks every new cue against them.

    Video stays video

    MOV and MP4 keep their picture, timecode and other audio tracks untouched. The levelled copy plays in Resolume, QLab or ProPresenter like the original.

    Stays on your Mac

    Nothing is uploaded and there's no account. Updates install themselves after you say yes.

    From folder to PA in three steps.

    1Drop the show

    Drag the cue folder in. Every file is measured as it arrives.

    2Check the plan

    See what will change in each cue, and why. Untick anything you don't want.

    3Level and play

    Press Level all. The copies are in your Levelled folder, ready for playback.
